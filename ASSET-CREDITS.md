# Asset credits

## Portfolio imagery (supplied)

All project visuals come from the two supplied folders and are processed by `npm run media`.

| Source | Contents | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `creative-portfolio/` (projects 01–10) | PNG concept stills and GIF camera-move loops | AI-generated art-direction concepts for self-initiated projects |
| `portfolio-projects-11-30/` (projects 11–30) | JPG concept stills and GIF camera-move loops | AI-generated art-direction concepts for self-initiated projects |

Derived files in `public/media/`:

- `work/<slug>/still.jpg`: re-encoded master at the original resolution (1536–1672 px wide, never upscaled).
- `work/<slug>/detail-1..3.jpg`: crops of the same still, picked automatically from the busiest regions.
- `work/<slug>/preview.mp4`: the supplied GIF, re-encoded to H.264 with no audio. It is labelled on the site as an "animated concept preview", not as footage.
- `breakdown/morrow-*.jpg`: crops of the Morrow still used in the home-page editing breakdown.

The colour palettes on case-study pages are sampled from each still (k-means over a downsampled copy).

## Generated by this project

- `public/icon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `icon-*.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`: the frame/playhead identity mark, drawn for this site.
- `public/og/og-*.png`: Open Graph cards rendered by `npm run og` from the headshot, the site copy and three supplied stills.
- `public/textures/grain.png`: a procedurally generated noise tile.

## Third-party assets

- **Fonts:** Fraunces and Manrope via `next/font/google`, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. They are self-hosted at build time.
- **Icons:** Lucide (`lucide-react`), ISC License.
- **Stock photography and video:** none. No stock or third-party imagery has been downloaded or used.
